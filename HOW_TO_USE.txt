HOW TO USE: motor-speech-overlay
====================================

PURPOSE
  Model clinical neurogenic and motor-speech variations (spastic, flaccid, ataxic,
  hypokinetic, hyperkinetic dysarthria, apraxia) as parameter modifiers interfacing with core profiles.

CAPABILITIES
  - Classify neuromotor disorder: Flaccid, Spastic, Ataxic, Hypokinetic, Mixed Dysarthria, CAS
  - Quantify laryngeal modifiers: glottal incompetence (hypoadduction) vs strain (hyperadduction)
  - Model resonatory subsystem: velopharyngeal port leakage (mm2) and nasalance score (%)
  - Calculate articulatory imprecision: stop spirantization rate and vowel space area compression
  - Detail prosodic modifiers: syllable segregation, rate reduction, and monopitch indices
  - Output machine-readable motor_speech_modifiers.json

USE CASES
  - "Model the speech characteristics of an individual with Down syndrome, Parkinson's, or ALS"
  - You need to apply a clinical speech modifier without rewriting the entire personality profile
  - You are developing communicative support tools or speech therapy diagnostic reports
  - Simulating authentic neurogenic speech in medical education or dramatic characters

IDEAS
  - Always keep voice-biometric-profiler and speaking-personality-profile as the spine
  - Apply this skill as a delta modifier on top of the baseline profile
  - Pass the motor modifiers to synthesis-control-compiler to adjust Klatt/Kokoro parameters

PROMPTS YOU CAN PASTE
  /motor-speech-overlay adult-male-30yo Down-Syndrome

  motor speech overlay for Parkinson's patient

  dysarthria profile of this stroke survivor

  clinical speech modifier for this subject. Model hypotonia and velopharyngeal nasalance.

  What motor speech adjustments are needed to simulate flaccid dysarthria on this baseline voice?

EXAMPLE FLOW
  You:   /motor-speech-overlay 30yo-male Down-Syndrome
  Model: 1. Neuromotor Classification: Mixed motor-speech variations secondary to hypotonia
         2. Laryngeal Subsystem: Closed quotient delta: -0.08, intermittent vocal fry on offsets
         3. Resonatory Subsystem: Velopharyngeal leakage: 12 mm2, nasalance score: 34%
         4. Articulatory Subsystem: VSA compression ratio: 0.82, stop spirantization: 32%
         5. Prosodic Subsystem: Speaking rate reduction: -0.8 sps, nPVI: 44.5
         [motor_speech_modifiers.json]

DEFAULT GOALS IF YOU DON'T NAME ONE
  1. Clinical dysarthria / motor-speech classification
  2. Laryngeal glottal modifier (adduction, perturbation, fry)
  3. Velopharyngeal nasalance and articulatory imprecision parameters
  4. Machine-readable motor_speech_modifiers.json block
